Outer loop · the playbook

Lessons in the prompt help when they fit, but don't reliably transfer

After each training drawing, an analyst reads the search tree and writes general lessons, e.g. "for a released oscillator drawn at an extreme, start from that pose with zero velocity". A selector gives each new drawing only the lessons that match its motion.

Helped where a lesson fit: the spring–mass gained in every run from the pendulum's lessons. Hurt where one didn't: the windmill got launch and bounce advice and turned "strobey".
