Our own metric fought the intent

"The sun scales up from nothing" can't start on the finished logo. Requiring frame 0 to match the drawing made the generator flash the whole logo, then reset. Each drawing now names its anchor frame: first, or last for build-ups.

intent Logo intro: the sun disc scales up from nothing, the rays pop in one by one around it, then the whole logo settles with a gentle pulse.
